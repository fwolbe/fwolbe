# Hi, I’m Fredrik

I work where product, company strategy and go-to-market meet.

Over the past 15+ years, I’ve helped software companies figure out what to build, who it’s for and how to bring it into the world. I’ve done that inside Microsoft during one of its fastest periods of growth, at Einride while the company scaled to becoming a Unicorn, and now as CEO of Fabella.

These days, much of my work starts with a conversation and ends with something tangible: a prototype, a product direction, a sharper market position or a tool that removes a week of manual work. I use models as collaborators throughout that process, from exploring a messy problem to pressure-testing a decision and building the first version.

I’m at my best when the map is incomplete.

## What I care about

- Finding the real problem before a team commits months of engineering to it
- Turning scattered customer signals into a product direction people can act on
- Connecting roadmap choices to adoption, revenue and a coherent market story
- Building small, useful things to learn faster
- Creating enough clarity for the team to move without waiting for permission

## A few chapters so far

**Fabella:** Reset the company around a focused customer and product strategy, built its first structured roadmap and shaped the move toward collaborative, model-assisted learning creation.

**Einride:** Helped define the company’s offer and market story across product, R&D, sales and marketing. Built the shared language and tools used to train 550 colleagues during rapid international growth.

**Microsoft Teams:** Worked across product, marketing and global sales as Teams for Education grew from 7M to 50M monthly active users during the pandemic.

**Microsoft Sweden:** Spent a decade close to products, partners and executive decision-making. Along the way, I doubled Windows tablet share in Sweden, delivered 165% of revenue target and served as Chief of Staff while the subsidiary became Microsoft’s best-performing market.

The scale changed. The work stayed surprisingly consistent: understand what’s shifting, make a choice and help people build around it.

## What you’ll find here
This is where I share experiments, prototypes and working notes from the overlap between product development, company building and applied AI. Some projects will be polished, while others will likely be a pile of crap.

I’m especially interested in:
- New ways for small teams to build and test software
- Products that collaborate with people instead of waiting for commands
- Better systems for product discovery, positioning, go-to-market and decision-making

## How I tend to work
I ask a lot of questions, write to clarify my thinking and build early enough to be proven wrong while it’s still cheap. I like ambitious ideas, but I’m suspicious of abstraction that survives only because nobody has put it in front of a customer yet. Resently I have moved to a show-don't-tell model of prototyping long term visions. Love it!
